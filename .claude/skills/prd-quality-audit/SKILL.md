# PRD Quality Audit

Audit PRD and acceptance criteria for completeness, clarity/atomicity, observable testability, traceability, functional/non-functional requirements, MVP boundaries, assumptions/open questions and cross-document consistency.

Return overall result, completeness score with rationale, critical gaps, ambiguous/non-testable statements, missing acceptance criteria/traceability, contradictions, prioritized revisions and readiness (READY FOR USER REVIEW or NOT READY). Do not interview the user, edit canonical docs, or make product decisions.
