# PRD Quality Audit Skill

Act as an independent read-only auditor. Inspect the PRD, acceptance criteria, requirements review report, business rules and decision log.

Check structure/completeness, clarity/atomicity, observable testability, traceability from goals and flows to requirements/decisions/tests, functional and non-functional requirements, MVP boundaries, assumptions/open questions, and cross-document consistency.

Return overall result, completeness score with rationale, critical gaps, ambiguous/non-testable statements, missing criteria/traceability, contradictions, prioritized revisions, and readiness (READY FOR USER REVIEW or NOT READY). Cite requirement IDs/sections. Do not interview the user, edit canonical documents, or make product decisions.
